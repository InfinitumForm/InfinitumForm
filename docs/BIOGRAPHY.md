# Biography

## Short biography

Ivijan-Stefan Stipic is a software engineer and software architect from Serbia with more than 20 years of professional experience across web development, backend systems, electronics, and industrial automation. His main work is in PHP, WordPress, MySQL, REST APIs, integrations, and the modernization of existing software. He has led engineering work on international platforms and contributed to public WordPress plugins. Alongside commercial software projects, he works on digital literacy and online safety education.

## Background

My career began in electronics. I worked with control systems, signal processing, communication interfaces, and industrial equipment before moving further into commercial web development. That background still shapes how I investigate software problems: I look at the complete system, not just the part where a failure becomes visible.

Over the years I have worked with agencies, product teams, companies, and nonprofit organizations. My responsibilities have ranged from writing application code and integrating external APIs to database design, performance work, technical decisions, and production support. My strongest production experience is in PHP and WordPress-based systems, but my work also includes custom applications and platform-independent integrations.

My work has also brought me into contact with people from sports, coaching, public relations, and business. Through professional projects and collaborations, I have worked with Ronnie O'Sullivan, Pete Cohen, John Di Lemme, Michelle Tennant, Drew Gerber, Sarah Moxom, Stefan Oberhauser, Natalia Yosco, Alain Claes, and Jenny Tan. Those collaborations have spanned the UK, Italy, Belgium, the Netherlands, the US, Hong Kong, and South Africa. The work and my responsibilities varied from one project to another; I mention these names as part of my professional history, not as endorsements.

Among the systems I have worked on are TOORLY, the OnlinePressKit247 and PitchRate platforms, enterprise intranet projects, and digital tools supporting child protection and online safety. I have also developed and maintained custom and publicly released WordPress plugins. These are contributions made in different professional roles, not products or companies I claim to own.

In addition to engineering, I have taken part in digital literacy and cybersecurity education, especially around privacy, online risks, and responsible technology use. I also have a longstanding interest in audio technology, independent publishing, and the relationship between technology and media.
